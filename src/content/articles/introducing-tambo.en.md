Apple (with Apple Upgrade) lets you “buy” the latest iPhone for just 32 USD a month for 2 years. Once that period is over, you can upgrade and keep paying.

The point here is that even after those 2 years, the phone still isn't yours. So, in other words, now even the product itself comes as a subscription.

These days, it's estimated that households pay for around [11 subscriptions in total](https://revelandrootsevents.com/cost/what-they-cost/), averaging up to [~$200 USD a month](https://www.crresearch.com/blog/subscription-service-statistics-and-costs/).

In a world where we pay subscriptions for absolutely everything and own nothing, I found that even keeping track of them becomes tedious.

When I moved out on my own, I started keeping track of my purchases and expenses, initially writing them down in Excel. Obviously, that didn't last, for the simple reason that the experience of doing it and keeping it up was shit.

I tried a bunch of apps that solved this problem. I'd quickly stop using them because they were either too complex, uncomfortable to use, incomplete or charged absurd prices.

Almost 3 years ago, tired of the situation, I took the more complicated path and decided to build tambo.

Keeping everything on your own device, with no AI, no subscriptions, no internet or ads. A one-time purchase and it's yours forever.

A simple, complete and intuitive app with one main goal: understanding where your money goes.

Installments, fixed expenses, expense groups, budgets, receipts, expenses in dollars or euros. These are some of the features tambo currently offers.

### Sources

The spending figures come from US surveys conducted in different years, rather than a current global average.

- [C+R Research (2022)](https://www.crresearch.com/blog/subscription-service-statistics-and-costs/): 1,000 surveyed consumers reported average monthly spending of USD 219 when itemizing their subscriptions.
- [West Monroe (2021)](https://www.westmonroe.com/press-releases/americans-are-spending-more-on-subscriptions-and-are-less-aware-of-spending): a June 2021 survey of 2,500 consumers found average monthly spending of USD 273 across 21 subscription categories.
- [Revel Roots](https://revelandrootsevents.com/cost/what-they-cost/) and [PostMonk](https://postmonk.co/blog/subscription-fatigue): secondary sources citing 11.2 subscriptions per US household and attributing the figure to Deloitte. That attribution could not be verified in a primary source.
